How to use your AI Coach

Step 1: Complete your Profile
Click 'Profile' in the top right. Enter your age, goal, target date, and exactly how many minutes you have available each day. Save it.

Step 2: Get your Initial Plan
Go to the 'AI Coach' tab. Select '1. Initial Plan' and generate the prompt. Copy and paste it into ChatGPT or Claude. The AI will verify if your goal is realistic based on your data, and generate a 12-week plan.

Step 3: Paste & Run
Paste the AI's JSON response back into the app and apply it (ensure 'Overwrite' is checked). Your 'Plan' tab is now populated.

Step 4: Log & Update
Log your runs daily. Every week, go back to the 'AI Coach' tab, select '2. Weekly Update', and repeat the process. The AI will analyze your logs and adjust the remaining weeks to ensure you hit your goal safely!
